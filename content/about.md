My name is Victor Li Chuang. I completed my PhD in Computer Science from the National University of Singapore
(Integrative Sciences and Engineering Programme) in 2025 under the co-supervision of
Prof. [Kan Min-Yen](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~kanmy) and
Prof. [Li Haizhou](https://www.eng.nus.edu.sg/ece/staff/li-haizhou).
Prior to the PhD, I finished my B.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering under the supervision of A/Prof [Thomas Yeo](https://cde.nus.edu.sg/ece/staff/yeo-boon-thye-thomas/).

I am currently an AI Algorithm Engineer at TikTok, working on industrial search and recommendation systems.

---

## Research Interests

My research interests include Conversational Recommendation Systems and Interactive Dialogue Systems.
